# DO IT UP Unisex Salon

DO IT UP is a unisex salon in Kothrud, Pune offering hair, skin, grooming, color, care, and massage services.

## Booking

Clients can request an appointment from the website by choosing a service, date, and preferred time. The salon owner reviews each request, adjusts the appointment duration if needed, and accepts the request before the client receives a confirmation email.

## Services

- Female haircut
- Male haircut
- Beard and grooming
- Wash and blowdry
- Global colour
- Highlights and balayage
- Hair spa and treatment
- Skin cleanup and facial
- Massage care
